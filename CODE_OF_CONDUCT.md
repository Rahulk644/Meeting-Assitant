# Code of conduct

My Assistant is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming project environment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate in issues, reviews, discussions, and community spaces.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent while still requiring evidence.
- Respect privacy. Never post transcripts, recordings, credentials, crash dumps, or personal data without
  the affected person's informed permission.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and respect licenses, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Help make the project accessible to contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate privacy violations,
credential sharing, malicious code, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact route in [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md). Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical and without retaliation.
